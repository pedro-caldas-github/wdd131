Curated European Rail Journeys

Discover our selection of unforgettable train trips across Europe. From high-speed city connections to scenic overnight journeys, find the adventure that suits your pace.

🇬🇧 London to Paris 🇫🇷 (The Quick Link)

The ultimate high-speed connection between two global capitals, passing beneath the English Channel in just over two hours. Ideal for business and quick city breaks.

  • Departure/Arrival: London St Pancras International ↔ Paris Gare du Nord
  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours 15 mins (Direct)
  • Stops/Highlights: Passage under the Channel Tunnel (Eurotunnel). No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (Standard Ticket): €60 - €120 (One-way)

Standard Class

Comfortable reclining seats, power sockets, and access to the Eurostar Café bar carriage.

🇨🇭 Interlaken to Lucerne (Panoramic Alps)

A stunning segment of the GoldenPass Line, offering continuous panoramic views of alpine pastures, crystal-clear lakes, and traditional Swiss chalets. A photographer's dream.

  • Departure/Arrival: Interlaken Ost ↔ Lucerne
  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours (Scenic Route)
  • Stops/Highlights: Passes through Brienz and the Brunig Pass. No long stops, focus is on the view.
  • Price Est. (Standard Ticket): €50 - €80 (One-way)

First Class

Spacious seats with large panoramic windows and enhanced meal service (optional).

🇦🇹 Vienna to Venice 🇮🇹 (Nightjet Sleeper)

Wake up in Venice! This comfortable overnight journey saves on a hotel night and maximizes sightseeing time, linking two historical UNESCO World Heritage cities.

  • Departure/Arrival: Vienna Hauptbahnhof ↔ Venezia Mestre/Santa Lucia
  • Duration: Approx. 10 hours 30 mins (Overnight)
  • Stops/Highlights: Graz, Villach. No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (Couchette Bed): €89 - €149 (Per person)

Sleeper Cabin (T3)

Private lockable compartment with three beds, sink, fresh linens, and breakfast included.

🇮🇹 Milan to Rome (Frecciarossa Speed)

Experience the speed of Italy's Frecciarossa 1000. Travel between the fashion capital and the ancient city of Rome in under 3 hours, connecting business and history seamlessly.

  • Departure/Arrival: Milano Centrale ↔ Roma Termini
  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours 55 mins (Direct)
  • Stops/Highlights: Bologna, Florence (Firenze). No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (Standard Ticket): €55 - €95 (One-way)

Executive Class

Dedicated staff, gourmet meal, wider leather seats, and reserved quiet carriage zone.

🇩🇪 Frankfurt to Brussels 🇧🇪 (ICE Link)

A vital European connection via the German Intercity-Express (ICE 3). Fast and reliable travel between Germany's financial heart and the capital of the European Union.

  • Departure/Arrival: Frankfurt Hbf ↔ Bruxelles Midi/Zuid
  • Duration: Approx. 3 hours 10 mins (Direct)
  • Stops/Highlights: Cologne (Köln). No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (2nd Class Ticket): €45 - €90 (One-way)

1st Class

More legroom, complimentary newspapers, and table service at your seat.

🇷🇸 Belgrade to Bar 🇲🇪 (Balkan Express)

One of the most spectacular railway lines in Europe, featuring over 400 bridges and 250 tunnels as it descends from Serbia's capital to the Montenegrin coast.

  • Departure/Arrival: Belgrade Centar ↔ Bar
  • Duration: Approx. 11 hours (Day or Night)
  • Stops/Highlights: Užice, Podgorica, numerous canyons and viaducts (no official stops for visiting).
  • Price Est. (Standard Seat): €25 - €40 (One-way)

Standard Seat / Couchette

Simple seating for day travel or budget-friendly couchette beds for the overnight service.

🇫🇷 Paris to Milan 🇮🇹 (Thello Night Train)

Travel overnight from the French capital to Italy's economic hub, passing through the Alps. A comfortable way to save time and arrive refreshed.

  • Departure/Arrival: Paris Gare de Lyon ↔ Milano Centrale
  • Duration: Approx. 10 hours 45 mins (Overnight)
  • Stops/Highlights: Dijon, Lausanne. No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (Couchette): €75 - €130 (Per person)

6-Berth Couchette

Economical shared compartment with six bunks, shared facilities, and basic comfort.

🇪🇸 Madrid to Seville (AVE Speed)

Ride the Spanish high-speed train (AVE) linking the national capital to the heart of Andalusia. A quick journey that connects vibrant northern culture to southern passion.

  • Departure/Arrival: Madrid Atocha ↔ Sevilla Santa Justa
  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours 30 mins (Direct)
  • Stops/Highlights: Córdoba (quick stop). No sightseeing stops.
  • Price Est. (Standard Ticket): €65 - €110 (One-way)

Elige Standard

Standard comfort level offering large, reclining seats, power outlets, and a dining car service.

🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿 The Jacobite (Glenfinnan Viaduct)

Often called the "Hogwarts Express," this famous steam train journey offers some of the most spectacular mountain and loch scenery in the Scottish Highlands, including the iconic Glenfinnan Viaduct.

  • Departure/Arrival: Fort William ↔ Mallaig
  • Duration: Approx. 3 hours 30 mins (One-way)
  • Stops/Highlights: Passes over the Glenfinnan Viaduct. Stop in Mallaig for lunch/visit (1.5 hours).
  • Price Est. (Standard Ticket): £50 - £80 (Return ticket, seasonal)

Standard Carriage

Traditional seating in heritage carriages, allowing open windows for photography of the scenic route.
